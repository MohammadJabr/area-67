# Earth elevation source comparison

Status labels describe evidence, not scientific suitability: **VERIFIED** means catalog item or local file inspected; **DOCUMENTED** means provider documentation supports the claim, but an exact local raster was not inspected; **NOT AVAILABLE** means the investigated named product does not cover the provisional coordinate. Exact item details and caveats are in [the CSV inventory](../../../data/metadata/earth_dem_source_inventory.csv).

| Site | Source candidates | Coverage / decision-relevant caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Haughton | Local Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (VERIFIED); ArcticDEM mosaic v4.1, item `29_29_2_2_2m_v4.1` (VERIFIED catalog) | ArcticDEM 2 m DSM item footprint includes point; 2009–2021 source span; 89 components. DEM, MAD, count, date and mask assets exist. Full DEM COG is about 1.05 GB, not acquired. ArcticDEM heights are WGS84 ellipsoid. |
| McMurdo | Local Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (VERIFIED); REMA v2.0 items `18_35_1_1_2m_v2.0` and `17_35_2_1_2m_v2.0` (VERIFIED catalog) | Both 2 m DSM footprints overlap the approximate point. Coverage does not choose a valley; source tile is ~1.0–1.14 GB each. REMA heights are WGS84 ellipsoid. Do not acquire until the intended valley is specified. |
| Askja | Local Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (VERIFIED); ÍslandsDEM v1.0 (DOCUMENTED) | National Survey of Iceland describes an open 2×2 m national DEM with mixed source data. Exact Askja tile, CRS, vertical datum, source dates and formal license were not verified. IS 50V is vector mapping, not a DEM. |
| Mauna Kea | Local Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (VERIFIED); USGS 3DEP 1 m bare-earth tile x86y220 (VERIFIED and downloaded); USGS 1/3 arc-second n20w156 (VERIFIED catalog) | The 1 m tile covers the point but point is only ~150 m from east edge. Horizontal CRS EPSG:26904; NAVD88 meters. The 1/3 arc-second item also covers point but is ~343 MB and was not downloaded. |
| Yungay | Local Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (VERIFIED); Atacama ALOS PALSAR DEM (NOT AVAILABLE at point); SRTMGL1 v003 granule S25W070 (VERIFIED catalog); CIREN contours (DOCUMENTED) | ALOS regional extent ends south of provisional point. NASA SRTM is a global lower-posting fallback with EGM96 heights, not downloaded. CIREN contours are vector data with access by request, not a ready DEM. Confirm exact field location before choosing a source. |

Nominal spacing is not effective terrain resolution. ArcticDEM/REMA mosaics are stereo-derived DSMs; their quality companion assets and varying source dates matter. Copernicus is also DSM. The downloaded USGS 3DEP product is a bare-earth topographic DEM, but structural QA does not quantify local uncertainty. Vertical references differ: Copernicus EGM2008; PGC ArcticDEM/REMA WGS84 ellipsoid; USGS NAVD88; SRTM EGM96. No cross-datum elevation comparison was made.
